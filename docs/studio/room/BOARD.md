# The studio room

We are one veteran studio making "אמא על הקו". Twenty senior people who have shipped games together
for thirty years, each the best in their discipline, each with opinions, all on one game. The owner
asked for exactly that: people who consult each other and build it together. This folder is how.

## Files
- `papers/<role>.md` — your position paper, ONE page. Post a first version within your first 20
  minutes of work, so others can react while you build. Update it when your view changes.
  Contents: what you found; the three biggest changes you will make or want made; what you need
  from which role; what you offer other roles.
- `threads/<topic>.md` — conversations. One file per topic, append only. Start each entry with
  `### <your role> — <HH:MM>` and address people as `@role`. Append with the shell (`cat >> file`),
  never rewrite someone else's text.
- `decisions.md` — the director's rulings. Binding. Read it before you start and at every milestone.
- `ROSTER.md` — every role, what it owns, and its agent id for a direct message (SendMessage).

## How we consult
1. Read the papers of the roles your work touches before you lock anything.
2. Anything that crosses into another role's area: open or append a thread, and send that role one
   short direct message pointing at it. Ask precise questions; propose, do not just ask.
3. Check `threads/` and `decisions.md` at every milestone of your work (roughly every 15-20 minutes)
   and answer what is addressed to you. A colleague waiting on you is your top priority.
4. Never wait idle for an answer. Proceed on your best assumption, write the assumption in the
   thread, and adjust later.
5. Disagree in the open, with evidence (a number, a screenshot, a rewritten line). The director rules
   when a thread does not converge: ask by direct message to `main`.
6. Direct messages are for decisions and blockers, a few sentences each. Long material goes in files.

## The standard
- Game of the year. The owner said: rewrite, replace, do not be afraid, only for the better.
  So anything may be replaced, and every replacement must be clearly better: say how you know
  (a lab number, a side by side, a colleague's review).
- There is a reason for everything. If you cannot say why something is in the game, fix it or cut it.
- Sharp, warm, original. Nothing borrowed from any other work.
