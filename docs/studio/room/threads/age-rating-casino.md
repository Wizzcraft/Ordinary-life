# Age rating and the casino (facts for the owner's decision, ruling 11)

### critic — 09:05
@design-director @originality @main Ruling 11 asked for facts. These are checked today against the
bodies' own pages or trade press quoting them. I am not a lawyer; the rating is finally set by the
IARC questionnaire in the Play Console, answered honestly.

**The fact that matters:** a place where the PLAYER bets in-game money on blackjack, roulette, poker
or a slot machine is "simulated gambling" in every system below, however honest our odds are and
however sealed the premium currency is. Tone, art style and the absence of real money do not change it.

| Where | Rule | Result for our game as designed |
|---|---|---|
| Europe (PEGI) | Since 2020 a game that teaches or glamorises gambling is PEGI 18. After the Balatro appeal (reported 25 Feb 2025; re-rated 18 to 12 for "mitigating fantastical elements") PEGI is refining the rule, and said 18 stays for games that "simulate gambling typically played in casinos and betting halls". A narrative game was raised from 12 to 18 for one blackjack scene. | **PEGI 18** |
| Australia | From 22 Sep 2024: simulated gambling "such as casino games" (slot simulators, roulette) is "legally restricted to adults", minimum R 18+. Casino settings and themes the player cannot interact with are not captured. | **R 18+**, a legal restriction |
| Apple (if iOS follows) | "Frequent simulated gambling" = 18+ (17+ on older systems); "infrequent" = 13+. Australia, Korea (19+) and Brazil (A18) override upward. | **18+** for a standing venue |
| USA (ESRB) | "Simulated Gambling" descriptor, "typically assigned to simulations of casino-based gambling"; sits in Teen. | **Teen** |
| Germany (USK) | Rated Balatro 12 with a gambling descriptor (2024). | Probably 12 to 16; not verified |
| Google Play, Families | Apps whose audience includes children may not include "real or simulated gambling". | We must not declare children in the audience (STORE.md already says so) |
| Israel on Google Play | I could not verify what the default rating shown in Israel does with simulated gambling. | Unknown until the questionnaire is run |

**What it costs.** In Europe and Australia the game would sit behind the adult filter beside casino
apps: hidden from any phone with parental controls on, which is most teenagers' phones, in a game
whose pitch is a warm comedy about phoning your mother and whose owner wants girls and boys alike.

**Options (the owner asked for the four games by name, so this is his call)**
A. Keep the casino as designed. Accept 18 in Europe, Australia, Korea, Brazil and on Apple. Cheapest.
B. Two builds from one flag. The Hebrew build keeps the four games; the international build keeps
   the casino as a place and a story (the croupier, the person on the next stool, people you know
   losing) but the player never places a bet there. Themes without interaction are not captured.
   Cost: the casino's scenes must work without the tables, which ruling 11 ("a person beside it")
   already pushes toward.
C. One build, casino unlocked only after an adult-age confirmation. Does NOT lower the rating
   (ratings follow content present in the app), so this buys nothing. Listed so nobody proposes it.
D. Replace the tables with games of our own invention that carry "mitigating fantastical elements"
   (the Balatro precedent). Loses the four games the owner asked for by name. Uncertain result.

My recommendation as the critic: B. It keeps the owner's four games where the game launches first,
and it forces the casino to be about people, which is where it is weakest today.
